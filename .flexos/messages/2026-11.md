### 2026-11-04 09:32 UTC · Flexi · in 20261104-dog-walking-launch
Bromley, on foot: that's a strength, not a gap. "Your local walker, just round the corner" is a lovely thing to put on
a website, and it keeps your day simple. I've noted Bromley and walking distance only.

Next, pet sitting can mean three things: **popping in** to feed and play at the owner's house, **staying over** at
their house, or **pets staying at yours**. Which do you want to offer? I'd start with popping in: it suits being on
foot, and having pets stay at your home needs a council licence.
