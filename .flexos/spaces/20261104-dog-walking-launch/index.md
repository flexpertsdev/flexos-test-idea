## What this is
The owner is starting a dog walking and pet sitting business from scratch in Bromley and needs a website for it. This
conversation shapes the business (where, what, how much), its brand and then the website.

## Decided
- Two services to start: dog walking and pet sitting.
- Where: Bromley, the owner's own local area only.
- On foot, no car: every walk and visit is within walking distance of home, and dogs are walked locally, never driven.
- The business needs a website, and a brand first; the owner is not creative, so Flexi proposes and they pick.
- The brand's colours: forest green, sunny yellow, cream (brand 1 on the board).
- The Library is set up: the project, the brand and the launch plan. Everything marked (guess) is Flexi's suggestion.

## Topics
- `01-brand/`: colours chosen; the name is waiting for a yes.
- `02-website/`: the home page, mockup v1 in its `files/`, waiting for a yes.

## Next
- The owner's yes to the home page, which also settles the name (Round the Corner Pets) and, for now, the prices.
- Still open: what pet sitting means. Pop-in visits are on the home page; to confirm.
- Then the agreed page goes into the website's brief and a build job builds it; decide where meet and greet requests go.
