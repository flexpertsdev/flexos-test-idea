## What this is
The owner asked for their home page. Flexi is drawing it here, in the brand, with the services and prices from the
Library. Mockups stay in this topic's `files/` (`home-v1.html`, then v2, …) until the owner says yes; then the agreed
page is written up properly in the website's brief (`apps/web/brief/02-pages/01-home/`: its `index.md` and the final
prototype) and a build job builds it from there.

## The home page, mockup v1 (`files/home-v1.html`)
- **The one thing to do:** ask for a free meet and greet; every button leads to the form at the foot.
- **Top to bottom:** top bar (logo, Walks and visits, Why local, Questions, Meet and greet); hero ("Your local dog
  walker, just round the corner.", two buttons, three trust marks, the owner's photo); how it works (three steps); walks
  and visits (four cards with prices); why local (on foot, small groups, same face, close by); about (the owner); five
  questions; the meet and greet form; footer.
- **The meet and greet request** (the page's one feature): name, phone and street needed; pet's name, what would help
  and anything to know optional; the owner replies within a day by phone or text. Where requests go (email, text) is
  not decided.
- **Checked** on a laptop and a phone (390 px, no sideways scroll).

## Decided
- One page to start: home, with everything on it (guess, Flexi's suggestion).
- The one thing to do on it: ask for a free meet and greet.

## Not settled in v1
- The name (Round the Corner Pets) and the prices are on it but not yet agreed.
- The trust marks (insured, DBS checked, first aid) may only go live once true: launch plan steps 3 to 5.
- Two photos from the owner (on a walk; a friendly face), their first name for the About heading, and the streets
  covered for the area answer.
