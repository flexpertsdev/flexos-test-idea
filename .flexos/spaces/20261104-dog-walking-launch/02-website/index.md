## What this is
The owner asked for their home page. Flexi draws it here, in the brand, with the services and prices from the Library.
On a yes the picked draft moves home to `apps/web/brief/02-pages/01-home/page.html`, with its `index.md`, and a build
job builds it.

## The drafts (files/)
- `home-draft-1.html`: one page with everything. The one thing to do: ask for a free meet and greet (every button
  leads to the form). Top bar; hero ("Your local dog walker, just round the corner.", trust marks, the owner's photo);
  how it works; four walks and visits with prices; why local; about; five questions; the meet and greet form; footer.
  Checked on a laptop and a phone (390 px).

## The meet and greet request, as drawn
Name, phone and street needed; pet's name, what would help and anything to know optional; the owner replies within a
day. Where requests go (email, text) is not decided.

## Decided
