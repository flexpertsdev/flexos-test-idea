## What this is
The owner asked for help creating a brand and a website and said they are not creative. So Flexi proposes and the
owner picks: three whole brands (name, logo idea, colours, fonts, a line), drawn side by side in `files/brand-board.html`.
What is picked moves to the Library (`docs/02-brand/01-overview.md`); this folder keeps only the board.

## The three on the board
1. **Round the Corner Pets** (Flexi's pick): forest green, sunny yellow, cream; "Just round the corner."
2. **Lead & Leaf**: pine, sage, terracotta; "Walks worth wagging for."
3. **The Bromley Pack**: navy and coral; "Tails up, Bromley."

## Decided
- Flexi proposes, the owner picks: no blank-page questions.
- The colours of brand 1: Forest, Sunny, Cream, Ink (in the Library's Brand).

## Next
- The name: keep Round the Corner Pets, which came with these colours, or see more names in this look. Flexi's pick:
  keep it.
- Then the website's look follows the brand, and the home page is drawn.
