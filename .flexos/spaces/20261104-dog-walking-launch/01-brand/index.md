## What this is
The owner asked for help creating a brand and a website and said they are not creative. So Flexi proposes and the
owner picks: three whole brands (name, logo idea, colours, fonts, a line), drawn side by side in `brand-board.html`.
Once one is picked it moves to the Library (`docs/02-brand/`) and to the website's look (`apps/web/brief/01-design/`);
this folder keeps only the board it was picked from.

## The three on the board
1. **Round the Corner Pets** (Flexi's pick): warm and neighbourly; forest green, sunny yellow, cream; "Just round the
   corner." Says local and on foot, and covers walks and sitting both.
2. **Lead & Leaf**: calm, a little upmarket; pine, sage, terracotta; "Walks worth wagging for."
3. **The Bromley Pack**: bold and local; navy and coral; "Tails up, Bromley." Best for Google, but sounds like group
   walks.

## Decided
- Flexi proposes, the owner picks: no blank-page questions.

## Next
- The owner picks one, or mixes (a name from one, a look from another).
- Then the brand goes to the Library and the website's design, and the home page is drawn.
