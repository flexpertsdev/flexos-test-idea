## The one thing to do
Ask for a free meet and greet (the form at the foot; every button leads there).

## What it shows, top to bottom
1. **Top bar**: the logo and name; Walks and visits, Why local, Questions; the Meet and greet button.
2. **Hero**: "Your local dog walker, just round the corner.", one line on walks and visits, two buttons (meet and
   greet; prices), three trust marks (insured, DBS checked, pet first aid), a photo of the owner on a walk.
3. **How it works**: meet and greet, walks or visits, a photo and a note after each.
4. **Walks and visits**: four cards with prices, from the Library's services and pricing.
5. **Why local**: on foot, small groups, the same face, close by.
6. **About**: the owner, with their photo.
7. **Questions**: area, insurance, keys, dogs that don't mix, cancelling.
8. **Meet and greet**: the request form (feature `01-meet-and-greet-request`).
9. **Footer**.

## Where it stands
Drawn (`page.html`), waiting for the owner's yes. Not built.

## Before it goes live
- The name is Flexi's pick, not yet confirmed.
- Prices and services are the Library's suggestions, not yet agreed.
- The trust marks (insured, DBS checked, first aid) show only once they are true: launch plan steps 3 to 5.
- Two photos are needed: the owner on a walk with a dog (hero) and a friendly face (about).
- The owner's first name goes in the About heading once known.
- The area answer needs the streets covered.
