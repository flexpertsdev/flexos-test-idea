Questions

Good to know

Which areas do you cover?

Bromley, within a short walk of where I live. Send me your street and I'll tell you straight away.

Are you insured?

Yes: public liability insurance plus cover for the pets and keys in my care. I'm DBS checked and pet first aid trained too.

How do my keys stay safe?

Keys are labelled with a code, never your address, and kept locked away. A key safe works too.

My dog doesn't get on with other dogs. Can you still help?

Of course. Book solo walks and your dog gets me all to themselves.

What if I need to cancel?

Free with 24 hours' notice. Later than that, the walk is charged.
