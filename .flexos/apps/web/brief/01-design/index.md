## The look
The Library's design system (`docs/02-brand/03-design-system/`): cream pages, forest headings and buttons, one touch of
sunny per section, white cards.

## On the website
- Generous space; one idea per section; sections alternate cream and white, and the About section is forest green.
- One main button per screen, and it always leads to the meet and greet.
- Photos are real (the owner, real dogs, local parks), never stock; rounded with one large corner, like the logo idea.
- Laptop and phone both: on a phone everything is one column and the top bar keeps only the button.
