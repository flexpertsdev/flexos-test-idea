## The look
The brand as the Library has it (`docs/02-brand/02-style-guide.md`, drawn in `03-design-system.html`): cream pages,
forest green headings and buttons, one touch of sunny yellow per section, white cards with soft shadows, rounded
everything. Fraunces for headings, Inter for text.

## On the website
- Generous space; one idea per section; sections alternate cream and white, and the About section is forest green.
- Every button is a forest pill; one main button per screen, and it always leads to the meet and greet.
- Photos are real (the owner, real dogs, local parks), never stock; rounded with one large corner, like the logo.
- Laptop and phone both: on a phone everything is one column and the top bar keeps only the button.
