## What it does
A short form on the home page: name, phone, street, pet's name, what would help (regular walks, solo walks, puppy
visits, pop-in visits, not sure), anything to know. Sending it reaches the owner at once, and the visitor sees "Thanks,
I'll be in touch within a day".

## Its rules
- Name, phone and street are needed; the rest is optional.
- The street is asked so the owner can say yes or no to the area straight away.
- No payment and no calendar: the owner replies by phone or text and arranges the visit (guess: enough to start).

## Pages it uses
- Home (`/`), its last section.
