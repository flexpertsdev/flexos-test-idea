## How we write (guess)
- Like a friendly neighbour: short sentences, "I" not "we" (it's one person).
- "Walk" and "visit", never "service".
- Name the dogs and the places ("a loop of Norman Park"), never "your pet" when we know its name.
- Calm and reassuring about keys and safety; never jokey about it.
