## Colours (chosen)
| Name | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#2F5D50` | the name, buttons, headings |
| Sunny | `#F2A541` | the paw, highlights, one thing to notice per page |
| Cream | `#FBF6EC` | backgrounds |
| Ink | `#1F2A27` | text |

White cards on Cream; never Sunny text on Cream (too faint to read).

## Fonts (guess: they came with the colours, not chosen yet)
- **Fraunces**, semi-bold: headings and the name.
- **Inter**: everything else.

## Logo (guess)
A rounded corner in Forest with a Sunny paw inside it, beside the name in Fraunces. Works small (the paw alone) for
social pictures.

## How we write (guess)
- Like a friendly neighbour: short sentences, "I" not "we" (it's one person).
- Name the dogs and the places ("a loop of Norman Park"), never "your pet" when we know its name.
- Calm and reassuring about keys and safety; never jokey about it.
