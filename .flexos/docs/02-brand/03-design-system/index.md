## Colours (picked from brand 1 on the board)
| Name | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#2F5D50` | the name, buttons, headings |
| Sunny | `#F2A541` | highlights, one thing to notice per section |
| Cream | `#FBF6EC` | backgrounds |
| Ink | `#1F2A27` | text |

White cards on Cream; never Sunny text on Cream (too faint to read).

## Type (guess: came with the colours, not picked on its own)
Fraunces, semi-bold, for headings and the name; Inter for everything else.

## Pieces
Buttons are pills: Forest filled for the one main thing, Forest outline for the second. Tags are Sunny pills. Cards are
white, rounded 18 px, with a soft shadow. All drawn in `design-system.html`.
