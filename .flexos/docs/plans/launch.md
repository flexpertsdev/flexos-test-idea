## What this is
Everything between today and the first walk, in the order Flexi would do it. Nothing here has started except the brand.

## Steps
1. - [ ] **Decide the services and prices** (`01-project/03` and `04` are suggestions to check).
2. - [ ] **Pick the name**, then check it is free: a web address, Instagram and Facebook.
3. - [ ] **Get insured**: pet business insurance covering public liability and the pets and keys in your care (guess:
   around £100 to £200 a year).
4. - [ ] **Get a basic DBS check**: clients hand over their keys, and showing it wins trust.
5. - [ ] **Do a pet first aid course** (half a day, online or local).
6. - [ ] **Register as self-employed** with HMRC.
7. - [ ] **Set up the paperwork**: a client form (vet, feeding, quirks, emergency contact), a key handover note, terms
   (cancellations, payment).
8. - [ ] **The website**: draw the home page, agree it, build it, put it live.
9. - [ ] **A Google Business Profile** as a service-area business (home address hidden), so "dog walker Bromley" finds
   you.
10. - [ ] **Tell the neighbourhood**: flyers through doors in the streets you cover, local vets' and pet shops'
    noticeboards, Bromley Facebook groups and Nextdoor.
11. - [ ] **First meet and greets**, then the first walks; ask each happy client for a Google review.

## Not needed (guess)
A council licence: only for pets staying at your own home (boarding), which is not offered.
