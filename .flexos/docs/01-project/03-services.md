All on foot, within walking distance of the owner's home in Bromley.

## Dog walking (agreed)
- **Group walk, 1 hour** (guess): up to 4 dogs that get on, picked up and dropped home. Small groups suit being on foot.
- **Solo walk, 1 hour** (guess): one dog (or dogs from one home) alone: for nervous, reactive or older dogs.
- **Puppy visit, 30 minutes** (guess): a short walk or garden play, a toilet break and a feed, for puppies not ready
  for long walks.

## Pet sitting (agreed; what it includes is not confirmed)
- **Pop-in visit, 30 minutes** (guess, Flexi's pick): at the owner's home while they are away or at work: feeding,
  fresh water, litter, play, a cuddle and a photo update. For dogs, cats, rabbits and small pets.
- **Not offered** (guess): overnight stays, and pets staying at the owner's own home (that needs a council licence).

## With every booking (guess)
- A free meet and greet first, at the client's home.
- A photo and a short note after each walk or visit.
- The same person every time.
