## Who (guess)
1. **Commuters**: out from early morning to evening (into London from Bromley South or Bromley North); their dog needs
   a midday walk on weekdays.
2. **Families with a new puppy**: need short visits a few times a day.
3. **Older owners**: love their dog, but long walks are getting hard.
4. **Holidaymakers and cat owners**: need pop-in visits while they're away, rather than a cattery or kennels.

## What they worry about (guess)
- Trusting a stranger with their keys and their pet.
- Their dog being one of a big group, or being driven around in a van.
- Not knowing how the walk went.

## What wins them (guess)
- A neighbour, not a company: local, on foot, the same face every time.
- Small groups or solo walks.
- A photo and a note after every visit.
- Insured and checked (see the launch plan).
