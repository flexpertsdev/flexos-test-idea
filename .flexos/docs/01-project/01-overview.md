## What it is
A new local business in **Bromley** offering **dog walking** and **pet sitting**: taking dogs out while their owners
are at work or away, and looking after pets at home when their owners cannot.

## How it works
The owner works on foot and has no car, so the business covers only their own neighbourhood: every client is within
walking distance, and dogs are walked in the local streets and parks, not driven to the countryside. Being close by is
the selling point: the same local face, nearby, every time (guess).

## Who it is for
Pet owners in the owner's part of Bromley who are out at work all day, go away, or cannot walk their dog themselves
(guess). More in `05-audience.md`.

## Where it stands
An idea, not trading yet (guess). Colours chosen; no name, prices or website yet. The steps to opening are in the
Library's plan `plans/launch.md`.
